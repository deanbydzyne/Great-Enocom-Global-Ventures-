# Project brief

## Purpose
A digital storefront and order-enquiry channel for GREAT ENOCOM GLOBAL VENTURES, serving catfish farmers, feed formulators, commercial feed producers, aquaculture businesses and finished-feed buyers.

## Products and services
Raw materials for catfish feed formulation; feed milling and pelleting; finished sinking-pellet catfish feed production and distribution; feed ingredients and nutritional additives. The existing catalogue contains 21 raw ingredients/additives and three finished feeds: 2 mm Starter, 4 mm Grower and 6 mm Finisher. Do not infer unprovided prices, weights, packaging sizes, feed analysis or guarantees.

## Storefront journey
Visitors browse, search and filter products; inspect details; add items to an order list; enter quantity/packaging requests and notes; review a summary; then send a WhatsApp enquiry or copy the summary. The business confirms availability, price, quantity and packaging. This is an enquiry, not an automated confirmed sale.

## Visual direction
Preserve the existing official logo and visual identity. Premium, clear, practical, trustworthy and mobile-first. Avoid clutter, generic AI agricultural imagery, excessive gradients and unnecessary animation. Preserve authentic supplied product imagery. Pellet illustration transparency applies only to pellets, never the background panel.

## Deployment
Netlify free static .netlify.app hosting. Prefer static architecture unless a future requirement proves a backend is necessary and the owner approves it.
